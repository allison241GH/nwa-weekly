## How I like it delivered
- I learn in many formats. Where a good **YouTube video, LinkedIn post or X thread** explains something better than a headline, include it.
- For each top item: what happened (1–2 sentences), why it matters to me, and a talking point or action **only if it's genuinely useful**.
- End with 1–2 **ideas I could act on**: a Medium topic, a startup to track, an FAU class talking point, or an OuchySport angle.
- No filler. If nothing new happened in an area, skip it.
- Don't let one big story spread across several sections.
