## Section A — Top Stories of the Week

### 1. Markets and Macro

July inflation cooled for a second straight month — headline CPI +0.1% (3.4% annual), core at 2.5%, and PPI flat — which knocked the odds of a September Fed *hike* down to roughly a third and kept the funds rate parked at 3.50–3.75%, even as a soft retail-sales print and a dour Michigan sentiment read tempered the mood; the S&P 500 tagged a fresh record Thursday before slipping ~0.2% Friday to close out a third consecutive weekly gain, with QQQ near $731. The next catalysts are Powell at Jackson Hole and the Aug 26 PCE print.

**Why it matters to Jamie:** As a long-term passive holder of S&P/QQQ/treasuries, this is a "stay the course" tape — cooling inflation supports the melt-up, but for **JEPQ/JEPI** a grind to new highs is exactly where their covered-call overlay caps upside versus the passive core, so the trade-off you're paid for (premium income now, capped rallies later) is live right here.

### 2. Anthropic backers target a ~$2 trillion October IPO — potentially the largest ever

Reports out Aug 13 (Fortune, Forbes, Qz) say Anthropic's *investors* are targeting a ~$2 trillion valuation for an October IPO led by Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, and JPMorgan — which would eclipse SpaceX's $1.77T June debut and rank as the biggest public offering in history. The figure is backer speculation, not a company-set target: Anthropic hasn't confirmed a timeline, valuation, or ticker, though its annualized revenue has run from ~$1B in late 2024 to ~$47B by May, with $100–120B projected by December.

- **Why it matters to Jamie:** As an angel investor and an Applied AI leader building daily on Claude, this is the defining liquidity event of the cycle — a $2T print resets the comp table for every AI valuation you'll see in a pitch, and it hard-codes how much pre-IPO value now accrues to insiders before public (or angel) money can touch it.
- **Talking point:** At a ~30x forward-revenue multiple the implied number tops $3T — a clean whiteboard prompt for whether AI-leader multiples are rational or reflexive.

### 3. AI coding-tools funding wave: Lovable ($400M) and CodeRabbit ($143M)

The AI software-development category drew two of the week's biggest checks: Sweden's **Lovable** raised a $400M Series C (Menlo Ventures, EQT, CapitalG, Palantir founders) at a reported ~$500M annual run-rate, and code-review platform **CodeRabbit** closed $143M (Atomico, Smash Capital, GV, BMW i Ventures, YC). Both sit squarely in the "agents that write and review code" lane that's now the leading enterprise AI use case.

- **Why it matters to Jamie:** As an Applied AI leader who lives in agentic coding tools, this is your category being repriced in real time — and as an angel it's a flashing "crowded field" signal: capital is pouring into dev-tools faster than durable moats are forming.
- **Suggested action:** When you next diligence a dev-tools seed deal, press on distribution and switching costs, not model quality — everyone has the model now.

### 4. Form Energy raises $750M for multi-day grid storage — the AI-power buildout's picks and shovels

**Form Energy** pulled a $750M Series G (T. Rowe Price lead, with Sequoia, Coatue, Janus Henderson, Franklin Templeton) to scale iron-air batteries for multi-day grid storage. It's the quiet infrastructure story underneath the AI boom: data-center demand is now a grid problem, and long-duration storage is one of the few ways to firm up the renewable supply hyperscalers are contracting for.

- **Why it matters to Jamie:** As an investor sizing where AI capital actually lands, the compute story keeps flowing downstream into energy — a durable thesis that touches the family portfolio's long-term energy and utility exposure, not just the chip names.

## Section C — Trends to Watch

### Humanoid robots cross from demos into real deployment
As of August, Figure's third-gen robot has passed 1,000 units in the field, AgiBot sits near 15,000 cumulative, and Boston Dynamics' electric Atlas deployments are underway in Hyundai plants — the story is shifting from launch videos to unit counts and paying pilots.
- **Why it matters to Jamie:** As an angel and a former product leader, the signal to track is *deployment velocity* (second use-case in weeks, not a year), which is the leading indicator that separates real embodied-AI traction from CES theater.

### AI is bending the entire venture capital curve
H1 2026 global venture funding hit a record ~$510B with AI capturing an estimated 35% of tracked capital, and the mega-rounds keep landing (Form Energy $750M, Firmus $2B, Lovable $400M). It's a boom with unusual concentration — a handful of names absorbing an outsized share.
- **Why it matters to Jamie:** As an angel investor, concentration is the risk hiding inside the euphoria — when capital and returns cluster this tightly, portfolio construction and entry discipline matter more, not less (a natural bridge to this week's pro-rata rights lesson).
