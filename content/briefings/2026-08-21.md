## Section A — Top Stories of the Week

### 1. Markets and Macro

U.S. equities held near records into a quiet pre-Jackson Hole week — the S&P 500 sat around 7,674 and the Nasdaq 100 clawed back an intraday drop to ~29,347 after the Treasury said on Aug 19 it would more than double buybacks of long-dated debt (from a $2B cap to at least $4B), pulling 10-/20-/30-year yields lower and cushioning stocks. All eyes now turn to Kevin Warsh's first Jackson Hole address as Fed chair on Aug 28, a chair who has deliberately stopped telegraphing moves ahead of the Sept 16 FOMC.

**Why it matters to Jamie:** As a long-term passive holder of S&P/QQQ/treasuries, this is a "stay the course" week — but the falling long-end yields lift your treasury sleeve's mark, and with QQQ pinned near highs your **JEPQ/JEPI** covered-call overlay is capping upside rather than earning fat premium, so their edge is deferred until volatility returns around Warsh's speech.

### 2. Groq raises $350M to become the "AI inference cloud" *(theme: ai-inference-cloud)*

Groq closed a **$350M Series A at a $3.5B valuation** (led by Disruptive, with NVIDIA participating), completing its pivot from chipmaker to cloud operator after licensing its core tech to NVIDIA and losing founder-CEO Jonathan Ross to an NVIDIA hire. It plans to scale from 57MW to 200MW+ of capacity next year across 13 data centers serving 6M+ developers.

- **Why it matters to Jamie:** As an Applied AI leader, this is the clearest signal yet that *inference* — not training — is where the durable AI business (and cost curve) lives; the tokens your agents burn are becoming a competitive, commoditizing market in your favor.
- **Talking point:** A sharp FAU framing — "the training race made headlines; the inference race makes money."

### 3. Higgsfield raises $400M as AI video finds its enterprise foothold *(theme: ai-video-generation)*

Higgsfield raised **$400M at a $5.4B valuation** (led by DST Global), quadrupling its value since January on the back of annualized revenue reportedly climbing from ~$20M to ~$700M in roughly a year as spend shifts toward enterprise customers.

- **Why it matters to Jamie:** As an angel investor, this is a textbook product-market-fit trajectory worth studying — a 35x revenue ramp is the kind of signal that separates a fad from a franchise (and it's the subject of this week's Venture Learning lesson).
- **Suggested action:** Add generative-video to your OuchySport content-cost model — enterprise-grade AI video is now cheap enough to change marketing economics.

### 4. Nevada clears Tesla, Waymo and Uber for paid robotaxis *(theme: robotaxi-regulation)*

The Nevada Transportation Authority approved autonomous-network permits for **Tesla, Waymo, and Uber** to run paid rides in Clark County (Las Vegas); reporting suggests Tesla was cleared to deploy up to 5,000 robotaxis over the next 12 months following the Aug 20 meeting.

- **Why it matters to Jamie:** Through your former-FordPass/CPO lens, a permissive state framework and a multi-operator green light is the regulatory inflection that turns robotaxis from pilots into a scaled consumer product — the market-structure question is now "who owns the fleet economics," not "will it work."

## Section C — Trends to Watch

- **Enterprise AI agents cross from pilot to production.** *(agentic-enterprise-workflows)* Gartner now projects 40%+ of large enterprises will have deployed AI agents to automate at least one previously-human decision workflow by year-end, with the real value in multi-agent orchestration that spans HR, finance, and procurement systems.
  - **Why it matters to Jamie:** As an Applied AI leader, the frontier is no longer single-prompt tools but orchestrated agent *teams* — the exact muscle you're building — and it's becoming the enterprise buying criterion.

- **Humanoids quietly cross the deployment threshold.** *(humanoid-robot-deployment)* Figure reports surpassing 10,000 partner-warehouse deployments (its second industrial use case took 30 days vs. 12 months for the first), while 1X opened consumer pre-orders for its NEO home humanoid with a 2026 delivery timeline.
  - **Why it matters to Jamie:** As an angel/board investor, the collapsing deploy-time curve — and a consumer SKU — is the leading indicator that embodied AI is nearing the venture-relevant "scaling" phase, not the demo phase.
