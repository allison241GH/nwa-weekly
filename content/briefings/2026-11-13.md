## Section A — Top Stories of the Week

### 1. Markets and Macro

After its worst week since the April 2025 tariff crash, the market snapped back — the S&P 500 rose ~2.5% to 7,561.67 and the Nasdaq added ~2.3% to 25,136.27 (its best week since August) — even as a Friday chip-led fade (Nvidia's sixth straight weekly drop, AMD −8.7%) trimmed the gains. The real story was rates: hotter-than-expected CPI/PPI pushed the 10-year Treasury yield up ~30bps to 5.15% and lifted December Fed-*hike* odds to ~85–90%, flipping the market's bet from "cut" to "hike."

**Why it matters to Jamie:** For your passive S&P/QQQ core the week's whipsaw is noise, but the rate repricing is the real variable — a December hike pressures long-duration growth more than the AI-capex scare did; and in a sharp rebound week like this one, JEPQ/JEPI's covered-call overlay caps some of the upside versus straight QQQ, which is exactly the trade-off you accept for the premium income that cushions the choppy stretches.

### 2. Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO after 33 years

Nvidia founder **Jensen Huang resigned as CEO, president, and board member effective immediately on Nov 12** — after 33 years — following allegations of extramarital affairs. CFO **Colette Kress** was named interim CEO with no succession plan in place; the board hasn't yet selected a search firm and has reportedly approached Arm CEO René Haas about the permanent role.

**Why it matters to Jamie:** As a board director, this is a live case study in the single most expensive governance failure there is — no succession plan at the most systemically important company in the AI economy, plus acute founder-dependency risk that every board (including NWA portfolio boards) should now stress-test.

**Talking point:** Make it your next board-meeting prompt — *"If our founder-CEO vanished tomorrow, what breaks in the first 24 hours?"*

### 3. Anthropic ships Claude Fable 5.2 (and Mythos 5.2) to all customers

Anthropic released **Claude Fable 5.2** on Nov 9 — currently the top-ranked model in the overall frontier top 10 — alongside **Mythos 5.2**, the same model without the cyber- and biology-safety guardrails, for gated use.

**Why it matters to Jamie:** As an Applied AI leader building and teaching with Claude, this is a direct upgrade to your daily toolchain and FAU material — and the Fable/Mythos split is itself a teachable artifact on how frontier labs are productizing safety tiers.

**Suggested action:** Re-run one of your heaviest Claude Code workflows on Fable 5.2 before your next cohort to see what the upgrade actually buys you.

### 4. OpenAI closes $11B — at a valuation *below* its ask

OpenAI closed the first **$11B tranche of a round valuing it at $1.25 trillion** (SoftBank, MGX) — below the **$1.4–1.5T it originally sought**, and only part of a target that may reach $20–30B.

**Why it matters to Jamie:** As an angel investor, the signal is the haircut — when even OpenAI accepts a below-ask valuation, private AI pricing is resetting, and the discipline you bring to NWA seed terms just got validated at the very top of the market.

**Talking point:** Anchor your next AI-deal conversation on ownership percentage and entry multiple, not the founder's headline valuation.

## Section C — Trends to Watch

- **AI's capex engine is meeting a new gate: public resistance.** Part of this week's selloff was driven by fears that community and permitting pushback on data-center expansion could throttle the buildout the entire AI trade is priced on.
  - *Why it matters to Jamie:* As an angel with AI-infra-dependent portfolio exposure, the binding constraint may be shifting from chips and capital to land, power, and local politics — a diligence question worth asking founders now.
- **Robotics is forking: generalist models vs. purpose-built specialists.** OpenAI disbanded its standalone robotics team this week, betting general models (GPT-6 "Astra") can drive robots directly, while humanoid specialists press on — Figure is now hiring overnight human "shift coordinators" to supervise its robots, and Swiss startup Embodied AI unveiled a factory-floor humanoid, "AIR."
  - *Why it matters to Jamie:* For a board director and investor, this is the classic platform-vs-specialist bet playing out in real time — and it reframes how to value any embodied-AI startup's moat.
