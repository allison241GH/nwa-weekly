## How to read this

These are **illustrative** covered-call candidates for the two positions Jamie writes calls against — **QQQ** and **F (Ford)** — built from this week's approximate prices and the published dividend calendar. They are **not live quotes**: premiums and deltas are estimates to frame the decision, so verify the actual chain in your broker before acting. The goal is always the **dual mandate — income plus a thin layer of share protection**: the premium is rent you collect and a small downside cushion; the strike is how much upside you're agreeing to rent out.

The backdrop this week matters. After a genuinely choppy stretch — an **OpenAI revenue warning** and open **AI-bubble warnings** from Dalio and Novogratz dragged the Nasdaq down midweek before a Friday rally pulled the indexes back to weekly gains — **implied volatility is elevated**, which means **richer premiums** than the calm melt-up of two weeks ago. That tilts this week toward *collecting* income at sensible strikes rather than reaching for tight ones, while leaving enough headroom to participate if the rally holds.

**Income vs. protection read:** Ford's ~4.9% dividend makes the static yields here attractive relative to the share price, but the dividend is also the trap. **Ex-div flag (REQUIRED):** The **Nov 9 ex-dividend date sits inside the Nov 20 and Dec 11 expiries.** The rule is plain — **never let a covered call sit in-the-money into an ex-dividend date you care about**, because an ITM call can be assigned *early* the day before ex-div so the counterparty captures your $0.15 dividend.
