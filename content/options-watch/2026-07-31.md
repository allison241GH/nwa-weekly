## F (Ford) — ~$14.63 (post-earnings; the event risk is now behind you)

Ford closed the week around **$14.63**, having popped ~5% after Tuesday's Q2 beat before fading back as the initial enthusiasm cooled. The key change from last month: **earnings are done.** Ford reported adjusted EPS of $0.42 (vs. ~$0.35 expected) and *raised* full-year adjusted EBIT guidance to $10–11B, and Citigroup upgraded the stock to Buy with a $20 target — so the tape is modestly bullish, but the binary earnings gap you had to write around in July is gone. One calendar item remains inside the windows: the **regular $0.15 quarterly dividend goes ex on Aug 11** (payable Sept 1). Ford's $0.50 strike grid is coarse at ~$14.63, so the practical OTM choices are **$15.00** (~2.5%) and **$15.50** (~5.9%).

The catch is the fresh bullish tilt: a Citi upgrade and raised guidance mean a drift toward your strike is more plausible than it was, so the 30-DTE $15.00 caps your upside close if the re-rating continues. If you want to keep more room for a move toward that $20 target, favor the $15.50 back-months.

**Ex-div / event flag (READ THIS):**

- **$0.15 ex-dividend Aug 11** falls *inside* all three windows. The rule holds: **never let a covered call sit in-the-money into an ex-dividend date you care about** — an ITM call can be assigned *early* the day before ex-div to capture the dividend. With OTM strikes ($15.00/$15.50) and Ford at ~$14.63, assignment risk is low *today*, but a post-upgrade drift toward your strike before Aug 11 flips that fast — be ready to roll up-and-out or close if Ford pushes into your strike ahead of the 11th.
