## How to read this

These are **illustrative** covered-call candidates for the two names you write calls against — QQQ and F — built from approximate, recently-observed prices, **not** a live options chain. Verify every strike, premium, and delta in your broker before acting; the numbers below are a framework for *where to look*, not a recommendation of a specific trade. Every candidate is out-of-the-money (OTM), targeting roughly **2–5% OTM** and a **~0.25–0.35 delta** (a rough probability of assignment). The goal is always the dual one: **income now + a thin downside cushion**, while renting out only the upside above your strike.

This week's backdrop flipped: a hot August CPI print has the market pricing a **~90% chance of a Fed rate _hike_** next Wednesday, the 10-year Treasury is near **5%**, and that jump in rate anxiety has firmed up implied volatility. For a call-writer that's good news — you're finally being paid a bit more to sell the same headroom — but it comes with a catch on QQQ, whose quarterly distribution goes ex-dividend **Sep 21**, now sitting just inside the front expiry. Richer premium plus a live ex-div date is exactly the combination where "reach a little closer to the money" quietly turns into "get your shares called away the day before the dividend."

**Ex-dividend flag — now the governing date.** QQQ's next quarterly distribution goes **ex-dividend on ~Sep 21, 2026 (~$0.55–0.69/share)**, which sits **inside all three windows** and just 8 trading days out. The distribution is tiny relative to a ~$716 share (≈0.08%), so early-assignment risk is **low while your strike stays comfortably OTM** — but the rule is absolute: **never let a covered call sit in-the-money into an ex-dividend date you care about.** An ITM call can be exercised the day before ex-div by a holder capturing the distribution, and you lose both the shares and the dividend. The 735 strike is the pressure point: if QQQ rallies back through it before Sep 21, roll up or close rather than carrying an ITM call into that date.

## This week's framing

The two names line up the same way this week, which is unusual: both are paying **richer premium** because volatility is up, but for opposite reasons — QQQ because the whole market is bracing for a rate hike, Ford because it has genuine single-name trouble. On **QQQ**, take the gift as _protection_: push out to the 60 DTE / 745 strike, bank the fatter premium, and stay far enough OTM that the Sep 21 ex-div can't touch you. If you insist on the front-month income, set a hard calendar reminder for **Sep 18** to check moneyness on any 735 call before ex-div. On **Ford**, the $14.5 strike is the sensible income line now that the stock has fallen, but size the trade as "rent collected on shares I'm holding through the storm" rather than a yield grab — and keep any November roll clear of the projected mid-November ex-date. Net: on QQQ, favor **protection/upside** (further OTM, longer-dated); on Ford, the **income** leg is defensible, but it's really downside-cushion income, not conviction upside.
