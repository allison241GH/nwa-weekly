## How to read this

This is a **framework with illustrative strikes**, not live brokerage data. Prices and premiums below are approximate, sourced by web search on **Sept 25, 2026**, and meant to frame the trade-offs — **verify live in your broker before acting.** Every covered call has a **dual goal**: the premium is *income* (rent on shares you already own) and a thin *downside cushion*, while the strike is how much *upside you're renting out*. Candidates target roughly **2–5% out-of-the-money (OTM)** and a **~0.25–0.35 delta** (a rough probability of assignment) across **30 / 45 / 60-DTE** windows.

- **Static yield** = premium ÷ price (what you collect if the stock goes nowhere).
- **If-called return** = (strike − price + premium) ÷ price (your total return if shares get called away).
