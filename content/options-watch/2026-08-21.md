## How to read this

These are **illustrative** covered-call candidates for the two positions Jamie writes against — **QQQ** and **F** — built from approximate prices sourced via web search, not a live options feed. Verify every strike, premium, and delta in your broker before acting. The goal is always the **dual mandate: income + share protection** — the premium is both rent and a thin downside cushion, and the strike is how much upside you're renting out. With QQQ sitting near records ahead of Warsh's Aug 28 Jackson Hole speech, implied vol is subdued, so premiums are modest and the case for *keeping some upside* is stronger than usual.
