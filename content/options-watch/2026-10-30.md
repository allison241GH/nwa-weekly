## How to read this

These are **illustrative** covered-call candidates for the two positions Jamie writes calls against — **QQQ** and **F (Ford)** — built from this week's approximate prices and the published dividend calendar. They are **not live quotes**: premiums and deltas are estimates to frame the decision, so verify the actual chain in your broker before acting. The goal is always the **dual mandate — income plus a thin layer of share protection**: the premium is rent you collect and a small downside cushion; the strike is how much upside you're agreeing to rent out.

The backdrop matters. For the second week running, investors sold AI-spending exposure — QQQ fell ~3.5% on Friday alone (its worst day since June) on Intel's cash-burn warning and Amazon's AI capex plans, and the Fed held at 3.75–4.0% with a hike back on the table. **Implied volatility is elevated**, which means **richer premiums** than the melt-up tape of a month ago. That tilts this week toward *collecting* income at sensible strikes, while the risk-off tone lowers the odds of an imminent melt-up that would run over your calls.
